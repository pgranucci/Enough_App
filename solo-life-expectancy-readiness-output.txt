## Readiness formula

```
readiness% = min(100, round(projectedGrossEquivalentAtRetirement / requiredPortfolioPV × 100))
requiredPortfolioPV = PV of year-by-year gross portfolio withdrawals from age 65 to lifeExpectancy
fundingYears = lifeExpectancy − retirementAge  (solo mode only)
```

## Key observations

- **Default (95):** 91% readiness; need $1,239,595; fund 30 years (ages 65–95).
- **Shortest (75):** 100% readiness (+9 pp vs default); required PV $655,175 ($-584,420 vs default).
- **Longest (105):** 82% readiness (-9 pp vs default); required PV $1,378,662 (+$139,067 vs default).
- Projected gross-equivalent at 65 is **constant** ($1,131,026) — life expectancy only changes required PV, not accumulation.
- First-year gross portfolio withdrawal is **constant** ($74,726) in this scenario (no SS); longer life adds more discounted out-years.
