## Tax model notes
- **Income tax:** federal + FL (no state income tax) on taxable gross each year.
- **FICA:** employee OASDI (6.2% to wage base) + Medicare (1.45%) on **work wages** only.
- Alex salary counts as work → FICA deducted from household net while Alex is still employed.
- Portfolio withdrawal G split: pre-tax portion = G×(1−s) is taxable; Roth portion = G×s is tax-free cash.
- Each year solves G so: known_gross + G − income_tax − FICA = lifestyle_net.
